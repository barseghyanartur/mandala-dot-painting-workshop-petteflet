What is a mandala?
==================

- A mandala is a colorful circle of patterns, like a flower or snowflake.
- The word comes from Sanskrit and means “circle.”
- It spreads out from the center, like ripples in water.

.. revealjs-break::
    :notitle:

.. image:: images/01_what_is_mandala.png
   :alt: Mandala
   :class: r-stretch
   :align: center

Where do they come from?
============================

- Mandalas have existed for thousands of years.
- They began in India and Tibet as a way to bring peace and calm.
- Monks and artists made them with sand, paint, or stones.
- Many cultures around the world create mandalas, each with its own style and meaning.
